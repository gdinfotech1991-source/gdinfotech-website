Discuss a board or component

Repair beyond module replacement

Chip-level repair means diagnosing and repairing individual electronic components on a circuit board, rather than replacing the entire board straight away. This can help investigate faults that affect power, charging, startup or other board functions.

Board-level services

  • Motherboard inspection and fault diagnosis
  • Power-delivery and charging-circuit troubleshooting
  • Component-level replacement, where suitable
  • BGA rework enquiries — BGA is a type of chip connection soldered beneath the component
  • Technical assessment of damaged or non-working boards

Individual components and spares

We also accept sourcing enquiries for individual components and replacement spares. Share the part number, board reference, component marking, a clear photo or the device model to help identify the correct item. Stock and sourcing are subject to confirmation.
